Privacy Policy

Effective 5 September 2026 · Last updated 5 September 2026 · 한국어

The short version. This app contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. It does not build a behavioural profile of you, does not access your location, contacts or photos, and does not sell personal information.

It does run its own server, for leaderboards and live duels, and it does contain optional rewarded ads. So some things are stored. Everything that is, and why, is below.

1. Scope

This policy covers the mobile application Ghost Sudoku ("the app") and its game server.

2. You can use it without an account

Solving puzzles, the daily challenge, statistics and achievements all work offline.

On first launch the app opens a guest session on the server automatically. That session holds a randomly generated account identifier and a display name, and nothing that identifies you. Signing in is only needed if you want your progress to follow you to another device.

What it does not store

  • From Google and Apple sign-in we receive only an account identifier and the name you chose to display. Your password is never given to this app.
  • Leaderboard results contain no cells and no digits. Only counts — how many cells were correct at each second. That was a design constraint from the start, so that another player's record can never become a route to the day's solution.
  • The app itself does not collect advertising identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or call logs.

4. What stays on your device

These never leave your device:

  • App settings — theme, language, sound, haptics
  • Your in-progress board and pencil marks
  • The replay data of your best runs, used for ghost racing
  • Whether you have seen the tutorial, and your notification preferences

Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications

Reminders — a streak about to break, the daily challenge being ready — are scheduled locally on the device. There is no push service, so no push token is created or transmitted. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

6. Retention and security

Passwords are stored only as hashes; the plaintext is never kept. The refresh tokens that keep you signed in are likewise stored only as SHA-256 hashes, and are destroyed and reissued on every use. Traffic to the server is encrypted with HTTPS.

Account data is kept while the account exists. Leaderboard and duel records underpin the standings and ratings, so on account deletion they are removed or retained in a form no longer linked to you.

7. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your information. Write to the address below and we will act on it.

  • Account deletion — ask by email and we will delete the account and the records linked to it.
  • Guest accounts — deleting the app removes what is on the device. You may also ask us to delete the server-side guest record.

8. Children

The app is not directed at any particular age group and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we learn that we have, we delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we update this document and the "Last updated" date above. Material changes, such as collecting something new, are also announced in the app.
